Under the hood

How it works

A normal webcam and mic, a hand-tracking model and a few careful rules. Everything runs on your computer.

🖐️

It sees your hand

Every frame, Wield finds your hand and 21 points on it, then compares the shape with the poses it knows. It only counts if one pose is clearly the closest.

MediaPipe hand landmarks, confidence and ambiguity checks.

〰️

It follows your moves

When you draw in the air, Wield traces the path of your fingertip or hand and matches its shape, however big or fast you draw it.

Path resampling and dynamic time warping. Swipes by direction and straightness.

💬

It listens for “Hey Wield”

After the wake phrase, Wield listens for your commands only, which keeps it accurate and stops random speech from triggering anything.

Offline Vosk speech recognition with a small grammar.

Get started

Up and running in a minute.

A normal Windows setup. No Python, no admin password.

  1. 1

    Download

    Wield-Setup.exe (about 130 MB) from the latest release.

  2. 2

    Install

    Double-click it and click Install. Wield gets a desktop icon and a Start-menu entry, and updates install the same way.

  3. 3

    Open

    Click the Wield icon on your desktop. A short tour shows you around.

Seeing “Windows protected your PC”? Wield is new and isn't code-signed yet (certificates are costly for a student project). Click More info, then Run anyway. You can read the code on GitHub, and every release is built and tested automatically there.

Rather not install? Get the portable zip, extract it and run Wield.exe. To remove Wield, use Settings → Apps → Installed apps.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need special hardware?

No. Any webcam works, including the one built into most laptops. Good lighting helps. For voice, any microphone works.

Is my camera or voice recorded?

No. The camera and mic stay off until you start a studio or turn on hands-free mode, and video and speech are processed on your computer and thrown away. Wield saves only what you choose to keep: the gestures you record (as hand-point positions, not pictures), your studios, phrases and settings.

What if I can't make the built-in gestures?

That's what Wield is for. Record any pose or movement you can comfortably make, or use your voice instead. In Settings you can make matching more forgiving, require a short hold, or add a confirm gesture.

Does voice need the internet?

Only once, to download the small speech model (about 40 MB) from Settings. After that, voice commands work fully offline.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

The download is for Windows 10 and 11. PowerPoint control and start-at-sign-in are Windows only.

Is it free?

Yes. Wield is free to download and use for personal use. You can read the source code on GitHub, but copying or reusing it needs permission.

How do I update or uninstall it?

Wield tells you when there's a new version. Download the new Wield-Setup.exe and run it: it updates in place and keeps your gestures, studios and settings. To remove Wield, open Windows Settings, then Apps, then Installed apps.

Something isn't working. What do I do?

Open Settings, then About, then Report a problem. It opens a GitHub issue with your version filled in. Attaching the log from the same page helps a lot.
